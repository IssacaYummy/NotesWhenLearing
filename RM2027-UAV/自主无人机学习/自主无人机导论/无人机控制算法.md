 自主无人机是一个感知-规划-控制的闭环系统
```mermaid
flowchart TB
sense["传感"]
mapping["建图"]
locate["定位"]
planning["规划"]
control["控制"]
fc["飞控"]
sense --> mapping
sense --> locate
mapping --> planning
locate --> planning
planning --> control
control --> fc
```

 底层飞控只控制推力和姿态，上层电脑中的控制器计算期望的推力和姿态
 参考的控制器开源代码链接： https://github.com/HKUST-Aerial-Robotics/Teach-Repeat-Replan/tree/experiment/onboard_computer/controller

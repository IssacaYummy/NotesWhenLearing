参考ardupilot官方文档,在没有GPS的情况下进行位置估计:

https://ardupilot.org/dev/docs/mavlink-nongps-position-estimation.html

文档解释了如何利用MAVLink向ArduPilot的EKF发送外部位置和速度估计，使其能够保持位置估计，从而无需GPS即可自我控制

# 飞控参数设置
想要使用外部里程计需要在飞控上修改一些参数
- AHRS_EKF_TYPE = 3
- EK2_ENBALE = 0 (若没有可以忽略)
- EK3_ENABLE = 1
- GPS_TYPE/GPS1_TYPE = 0 (禁用GPS)
-  [VISO_TYPE](https://ardupilot.org/sub/docs/parameters.html#viso-type "(in Sub)") = 1 (使用MAVLink)
-  [VISO_POS_X](https://ardupilot.org/sub/docs/parameters.html#viso-pos-x "(in Sub)"), [VISO_POS_Y](https://ardupilot.org/sub/docs/parameters.html#viso-pos-y "(in Sub)"), [VISO_POS_Z](https://ardupilot.org/sub/docs/parameters.html#viso-pos-z "(in Sub)")  (里程计距离机体中心xyz轴的偏移)
-  [EK3_SRC1_POSXY](https://ardupilot.org/sub/docs/parameters.html#ek3-src1-posxy "(in Sub)") = 6 (ExternalNav)
-  [EK3_SRC1_VELXY](https://ardupilot.org/sub/docs/parameters.html#ek3-src1-velxy "(in Sub)") = 6 (ExternalNav)
-  [EK3_SRC1_POSZ](https://ardupilot.org/sub/docs/parameters.html#ek3-src1-posz "(in Sub)") = 6 (ExternalNav) or 1 (Baro)
-  [EK3_SRC1_VELZ](https://ardupilot.org/sub/docs/parameters.html#ek3-src1-velz "(in Sub)") = 6 (ExternalNav) 
-  [EK3_SRC1_YAW](https://ardupilot.org/sub/docs/parameters.html#ek3-src1-yaw "(in Sub)") = 6 (ExternalNav)

也可以参考:
